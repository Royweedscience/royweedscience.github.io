Electrocution + Roller Crimper

Field setup for my non-chemical cover crop termination trial. Organic growers can’t use herbicides to kill cover crops, and the crimper alone often leaves survivors. Here is how we ran the Zasso electrical weeder and the roller crimper in the plots.

  • Cereal rye · soft dough
  • College Station, TX · 2026
  • Same-day passes
Electrocution (EWC)

Zasso electrical weeder

Unit
Zasso EH60, 2.5 m applicator
Speed
2 mph (3.2 km h⁻¹), one pass
Output
255 V, 40–45 A per phase
Energy
138–155 MJ ha⁻¹
Roller crimper (RC)

Roller crimper

Unit
Commercial roller crimper
Width
3 m
Pass
One pass

What we found

Used together, in either order, electrocution and the crimper terminated 80–90% of the rye at 28 days after treatment. The crimper alone reached 65% and electrocution alone 20%.
